Privacy policy

for the app “Miezelei” (iOS and Android) and the website miezelei.github.io

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. In short

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, your email address or your location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The friends leaderboard is optional. Only if you join do we use Google Firebase (database in Frankfurt am Main, Germany).
  • The app is funded by ads from Google AdMob. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent and only if you are 16 or older.
  • Purchases are handled exclusively by Apple or Google. We never receive payment details.
  • We don't use any analytics or statistics services, and we don't sell data.

3. Data on your device

The app stores on your device:

  • your game progress, e.g. coins, cats (including names you gave them), café, puzzles and streaks, progress in the arcade games, purchase numbers and the time of installation,
  • your settings, e.g. language, sounds, vibration and reminders,
  • whether you are 16 or older – your year of birth itself is not stored,
  • if you join the leaderboard: your anonymous sign-in and a cache of your friends' most recently loaded results,
  • your ad consent, which Google's consent dialog stores (see section 5).

The app reads your device language only to show German or English. This data does not leave your device except as described below. Backups of your device (e.g. iCloud or Google backup) may include it; Apple or Google are responsible for those.

You can delete everything at any time directly in the app: in your profile under Legal (“Delete my data”). If you only delete the app, the data on your device is gone, but leaderboard data on the server is not (see section 4).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the game) and Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (storage on your device that is strictly necessary).

4. Friends leaderboard, invitations and anonymous comparison (Google Firebase)

The leaderboard is optional. Only when you choose a player name there and join does the app connect to Google Firebase (provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Before that, there is no contact with Firebase.

The following data is processed:

  • a random, anonymous user ID (sign-in without email, password or name),
  • the player name you chose and your friend code,
  • your friendships (created only via the friend code),
  • your results: for the daily puzzle your points, number of guesses and puzzle language; for Cat Sudoku your highest level and stars,
  • a timestamp to compare with the server time (protection against changed clocks, so results stay fair),
  • if you enter a friend's code as a new player: an invitation (both of your IDs, the time, whether you then solved a puzzle, whether the reward was collected) and how many invitations were rewarded,
  • technically necessary connection data such as your IP address.

Only your friends see your player name and your results. There is no public list. With everyone else you are only compared anonymously (“better than X %”); for this your results are stored without a name. Who invited you, or whom you invited, is visible only to the two of you.

The database (Cloud Firestore) is located in data centres in Frankfurt am Main (region europe-west3). The anonymous sign-in (Firebase Authentication) is a global Google service; processing in the USA is possible. Google acts as our processor. For transfers to the USA, Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the EU Standard Contractual Clauses also apply.

We don't use Google Analytics or Firebase statistics.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the features you use); for the server time comparison Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in fair results).

Storage period: Daily puzzle results are deleted after 30 days. Everything else is kept until you delete it in your profile under Legal (“Delete my data”). We then delete your profile, player name, friend code, friendships (in both directions), results, invitations and the anonymous sign-in. If you delete the app without using “Delete my data” first, your profile and Sudoku result remain on the server. In that case please write to us (section 12), ideally with your player name or friend code, and we will delete them.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep Miezelei free, we show ads from Google AdMob (provider: Google Ireland Limited, address as above): optional ads that you start yourself in return for a reward, and occasionally ads between levels in the arcade games. There are no ads in the daily puzzle and no banners.

Consent: On first launch we first ask for your year of birth. Then Google's consent dialog (in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) asks whether you allow personalised ads. If you are 16 or older, on iPhones Apple then asks whether the app may track you across apps. Only after that are any ads loaded at all. You can change your decision at any time: in your profile under Legal (“Privacy settings (ads)”) or in your device settings.

With consent (16 and older): Google may use your device's advertising ID, device and usage data and your IP address (and an approximate location derived from it) to show relevant ads and measure them.

Without consent or under 16: only non-personalised or limited ads are shown, and there is no tracking request. For users under 16 we also mark ad requests as “under the age of consent”. Anyone who skips the age question is treated as under 16. Google still processes technical data (e.g. IP address, device type), for example to deliver ads, prevent fraud and limit how often an ad appears.

Google's ad component in the app also collects diagnostic data about itself (e.g. crashes and performance). We exclude ads intended only for teenagers or adults.

Ad-Free Pack: After purchase, we no longer show ads between levels. Optional ads for rewards remain available, but only if you start them yourself.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) TDDDG (consent); for non-personalised ads Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in funding the free game). Data may be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. In-app purchases and app stores (Apple, Google)

Purchases are handled exclusively by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The app only receives from the store which product was bought, a purchase number and the time of purchase. So that each purchase is credited exactly once, the app stores the purchase numbers in your game progress on your device, not on a server. We do not receive your name, your Apple or Google account or your payment details. With “Restore purchases” the app asks the store for your permanent purchases.

When you download the app and make purchases, Apple or Google process their own data under their own responsibility. Their privacy policies apply: Apple (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (purchase contract).

7. Reminders, ratings, sharing

  • Reminders are scheduled only on your device. There is no server and no push service for them. We only ask after you have solved a puzzle. A reminder may contain the name of one of your cats. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.
  • The rating prompt comes from Apple or Google. We don't learn whether or how you rate the app.
  • If you share a result (without the solution word) or your friend code, you use your device's share function. It goes to the app you choose; Miezelei itself transmits nothing.

8. Contact by email

If you write to us (e.g. via “Send feedback” in your profile), we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply. “Send feedback” adds a short technical line to the email (app version, operating system and language); you can see it before sending and delete it. The mailbox is operated by Google (Gmail).

We delete your message 12 months after your request has been completed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR (handling your request).

9. Crash reports

We don't use our own crash reporting service. If you allow sharing analytics data with app developers in your device settings, Apple or Google provide us with anonymised crash reports.

10. Website

The website miezelei.github.io is hosted on GitHub Pages (provider: GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in order to deliver the site and protect it against attacks. The website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no fonts or content from other providers. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure provision of the website).

11. Children and teenagers

Miezelei is meant for all ages but is not specifically designed for children under 13. Anyone younger than 16 receives only non-personalised ads in the app, and the app does not ask for tracking. If you are a parent and believe we process data of your child, please write to us. We will then delete it.

12. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

Because we don't keep accounts, we can often only identify you by your friend code. Please include it in your request (you'll find it in the app in your profile). Write to us at miezelei.support@gmail.com.

13. Information for users outside the EU

Miezelei is available worldwide. We do not sell personal data for money. Using Google AdMob may count as “sharing” data for advertising purposes under some laws (e.g. in California). You can opt out at any time by declining personalised ads in the app or by turning off tracking for Miezelei in your device settings. For requests under the law of your country, contact us at miezelei.support@gmail.com.

14. Changes

If the app changes, we will update this policy. The version published in the app and on miezelei.github.io applies.
